# FinWise Product Rules

## Product identity

FinWise AI is a financial intelligence system that helps users understand their money, learn patterns, explain financial situations, predict futures, simulate life decisions, and make better decisions.

## Core pillars

1. Financial Digital Twin
2. FinWise Copilot
3. Safe-to-Spend Engine
4. Financial Time Machine
5. Life Event Lab
6. Decision Intelligence

## Product principles

- Build production-quality foundations.
- Do not create fake buttons, meaningless placeholders, or fake AI experiences.
- Do not hardcode financial logic into UI components.
- Do not put API keys in frontend code.
- Do not tightly couple the app to a single AI provider.
- Keep the architecture extensible and clean.
- Prioritize correctness, maintainability, security, accessibility, responsiveness, and UX.
- Use deterministic logic for financial calculations and keep AI to explanation and recommendations.
- Keep the app approachable for beginners and powerful for advanced users.
- Support progressive intelligence and explain every number.

## UI principles

- Clean premium design with minimal clutter.
- Strong visual hierarchy and progressive disclosure.
- No generic banking-dashboard styling.
- Clear pathways for safe-to-spend, decision mode, time machine, and life-event analysis.
- Responsive design for mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop.
- Accessibility-first semantics and keyboard-friendly interactions.

## Architecture principles

- Separate pages, components, layouts, features, hooks, services, types, utilities, config, intelligence, and AI layers.
- Create interfaces and adapter boundaries for future AI providers.
- Design data models around currency codes and extensible financial records.
- Keep security and privacy at the center of the product.
- Do not expand beyond the current phase unless the foundation is solid.

## Development phase guardrails

- Phase 1 is a foundation only.
- No fake data presented as real user data.
- No unpaid or paid external provider required during this phase.
- Major features must have architecture behind them even if UI shells are present.
- Any unimplemented feature must have a clean extension point.
